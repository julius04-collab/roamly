    Questions

    Good to know.

    How does the trip-planning process work?

    Browse, send a short enquiry, and a planner replies with two or three options. You refine your favourite together before anything is confirmed.

    Are flights included in the package price?

    No. Starting prices exclude international flights unless a package says otherwise.

    Can I customize a trip?

    Yes. Change the length, swap activities or upgrade your stay. Changes can affect the price.

    Can I travel with a group of friends?

    Yes. Choose "Friends & Group Trips" and tell us the group size. Shared villas and riads work well for groups.

    How far in advance should I plan?

    Two to four months gives the most choice, especially in peak season. Shorter notice can still work.

    What happens after I submit an enquiry?

    A planner would email you options and questions. Nothing is booked or charged. On this prototype the form confirms, but nothing is sent.

    Are the prices fixed?

    No. Prices here are illustrative. For a real trip, availability, pricing and cancellation terms are confirmed in writing before you pay.
